• On 10.134.170.49 (jump-host), open PowerShell as Administrator and run:


  netsh interface portproxy add v4tov4 `
    listenaddress=10.134.170.49 listenport=2222 `
    connectaddress=192.168.5.10 connectport=22


  New-NetFirewallRule `
    -DisplayName "SSH forwarding to 192.168.5.10" `
    -Direction Inbound -Protocol TCP -LocalPort 2222 -Action Allow


  Verify the forwarding rule:


  netsh interface portproxy show all
  Test-NetConnection 192.168.5.10 -Port 22


  Then connect from your original computer:


  ssh -p 2222 guest@10.134.170.49


  This avoids logging into the Windows jumpbox, though network traffic still  passes through it.
